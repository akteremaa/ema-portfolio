/ 06 · How I Work

The engagement, from first message to handoff.

Most freelance projects fail not on skill but on process. Here is exactly what working together looks like, end to end. Promises kept. Surprises minimized. Documentation handed over.

01 Day 1-2

Scope & voice locked in writing.

Every engagement starts with a 30 minute call and a written scope on Upwork. For social retainers, voice and visual style guidelines get documented upfront so the work reads as the brand from post one, not after three weeks of corrections.

  • Discovery call (30 min, recorded with permission)
  • Written scope with milestone or retainer terms
  • Voice and visual style doc for content work
  • Shared Notion or Slack channel for project comms
02 Week 1

Audit then plan.

Before any content goes live, current state gets documented: existing posts, engagement patterns, top performers, and the gaps. The plan covers the first 30 days of content with hooks, captions, and asset briefs approved before publishing.

  • 90-day social performance audit
  • Content calendar for month one
  • Pillar mix across awareness, education, social proof, offer
  • Approval checkpoint before first post
03 Week 2 onward

Ship with rhythm.

Posts go live on schedule, engagement happens daily, and a weekly performance read flags what is working and what to cut. Async-first communication so the founder is not stuck on calls during their actual work hours.

  • Posts scheduled and shipped on cadence
  • Daily engagement on comments and DMs
  • Weekly written summary on Upwork
  • Loom recordings for anything that needs context
04 Month 1+ · Ongoing

Report, iterate, or hand over.

Monthly performance report with the data that matters and next-month recommendations. For fixed-price scopes, final delivery includes the operating doc so the team can keep posting after the engagement ends. No vendor lock-in.

  • Monthly performance report (one page)
  • Next-month content plan or scope review
  • Operating doc for in-house handoff if needed
  • Optional ongoing retainer if it makes sense

/ 08 · FAQ

Before you hire.

The eight questions every prospect asks in the first Upwork message. Answered here so the chat can move straight to scope.

What time zone do you work in, and how does that affect collaboration?

I am based in Khulna, Bangladesh, which is UTC+6. That puts me 11 hours ahead of US Eastern, 9 hours ahead of New York during DST, and 4 to 5 hours ahead of most of Europe.

In practice this works in your favor for async work. You go to bed, I work, you wake up to progress. For live calls I keep open windows at 6-10 AM and 8-11 PM Khulna time, which covers US morning and European afternoon. I respond to messages within 24 hours, usually within 6 to 8 hours during your working day.

How do payments work? Are you on Upwork or do you invoice direct?

All engagements run through Upwork escrow for both parties' protection. Fixed-price milestones for productized scopes, hourly tracking for retainer work. Payments processed in USD on Upwork's schedule.

I do not invoice direct, accept crypto, or take work outside Upwork. The platform fee is worth the dispute protection and the public review trail.

What happens when scope expands mid-project?

Scope creep is the most common reason freelance projects sour. I handle it in two ways:

For fixed-price work, the initial Upwork milestone defines what is in. Anything outside that scope gets a written change request with new milestone and price before any work happens. No surprises in the final invoice.

For hourly retainer work, weekly capped hours are agreed in advance. If we hit the cap, I flag it before continuing.

Will you sign an NDA? Can I share private customer data?

Yes, I sign NDAs as part of onboarding when requested. For sensitive customer data (PII, payment info, health records), I prefer working with anonymized samples wherever possible. If raw production data is needed, we agree storage and deletion terms in writing before the data leaves your environment.

I do not retain client data after engagement ends unless explicitly part of a retainer.

How do you handle revisions and feedback rounds?

Two revision rounds included in every fixed-price scope by default. First round catches the structural feedback. Second round polishes. Additional rounds are quoted as small change orders.

Feedback works best when consolidated. One detailed message beats six small ones. I document open questions in a shared Notion or doc so nothing gets lost in chat.

Do you white-label? Can I resell your work under my agency brand?

Yes. White-label engagements run the same as direct ones, with the agreement that I do not name your client publicly without permission and you handle all direct client communication. Most of my work for agencies runs this way already.

What is your typical turnaround for a social media retainer ramp?

For a social media retainer, the first month is the ramp: week 1 is brand voice and visual style alignment, week 2 is content calendar build and first posts going live, weeks 3 and 4 are rhythm setting. By month two the operation runs on autopilot from the founder's side.

For video editing, individual pieces ship in 48 to 72 hours from raw footage. Sequence work like a 5-email welcome flow ships in 1 to 2 weeks. Affiliate program builds run 3 to 4 weeks. Timelines are agreed in writing on Upwork before the first milestone funds.

What happens after delivery? Do you offer ongoing support?

Every fixed-price scope includes 14 days of post-launch support covering bug fixes and minor tweaks at no additional cost. Beyond that, you can engage me on the virtual assistant retainer (from $10/hr) for ongoing iteration, or come back with a fresh fixed-price scope.

I leave detailed documentation so your team or another freelancer can take over without me. That is part of the deliverable, not an upsell.
